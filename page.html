Formance

Senior capstone · Real-time fitness form correction · 2025

Form correction from a plain webcam — no wearables, no gym equipment. Frames stream over a WebSocket to a FastAPI backend, where MediaPipe extracts 33 pose landmarks, EfficientNetV2-S pulls spatial features, and a vision transformer reads across frames to classify which phase of the movement the lifter is in. PyTorch scores every rep 0–100 on depth, stability, posture alignment and knee bend, and the result returns as JSON to a React UI that updates live. Sessions roll up into a dashboard with rep counts, 30-day trends and per-metric breakdowns.

Trained on a hybrid dataset of public and self-recorded video. Roughly 60% of frames were the bottom of a rep, so class weights were applied to keep the transitional phases learnable, and learning-rate scheduling was added to stop validation accuracy oscillating between epochs.
